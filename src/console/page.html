What happens on run

  1. 01

    Read locally

    Every page is hashed and read on this machine. No page, crop, or text leaves it.

  2. 02

    Group into patients

    Pages are matched to a patient by MRN and bed. A page that matches nothing is held, not assigned.

  3. 03

    Refuse instead of guess

    An unreadable page is refused and named in the gap list. Every figure in the handoff carries its source page.

  4. 04

    Stay interruptible

    The kill switch writes a real STOP file the run polls between pages and stages. No partial handoff is written.
